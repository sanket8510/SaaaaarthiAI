About Saarthi AI

Saarthi AI is an autonomous, open-source multi-agent research assistant designed to replace hours of manual web surfing. It orchestrates distributed AI agents to plan research hypotheses, query search engines, read and verify articles, synthesize findings, and format structured reports.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions
Common queries about Saarthi AI
Is my data private? ▼
Yes. The backend server runs completely on localhost. Only your model and search API calls go to Groq and Tavily — no intermediate third-party logs your queries.
Why does research take 2 to 5 minutes? ▼
Unlike simple chat models that hallucinate quick answers, Saarthi AI performs real-time searches across several distinct URLs, reads each article in full, compresses notes, and synthesizes a cited report.
How do I prevent rate limit errors? ▼
Keep the Concurrent Units setting at 1 on Groq free tier. If a rate limit triggers, wait 60 seconds and run again.
How is the PDF generated? ▼
The PDF is generated client-side inside your browser via jsPDF. It formats titles, tables, quotes, bullet points, and adds branded header/footer elements.
